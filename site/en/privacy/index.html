ScholarReel stores research material on your device and connects to services you select when you use search and generation features. This policy explains how the app handles that material and how you can manage it.

Information stored on your device

The app stores papers and abstracts, generated briefs, research topics and keywords, sources, model and proxy settings, favorites, reading status, notes, task history and Trash records. These support reading, searching, generation and filtering repeated recommendations.

Model API keys and proxy passwords are stored in Apple Keychain using device-only settings with iCloud Keychain synchronization disabled. Keys are visible while you type them. After saving, ordinary configuration read endpoints do not return stored keys.

The current app requires no separate account, has no developer-operated research-data sync service, and includes no advertising or usage analytics SDK. App data may still be affected by the system device backup options you choose.

Sources, model providers and other external services

  • Paper sources: Your device requests paper lists or sends search terms according to the selected source’s interface. Sources include arXiv, Hugging Face and other sources you configure. Opening an original paper or PDF connects to its website.
  • Model providers: Search-term generation sends your topic title and keywords. Relevance screening and subcategory generation send topic information, paper titles and abstracts, paper identifiers and existing categories. Brief generation sends the paper’s title, abstract, topic, source link and instructions such as the target language. Your device sends these inputs and your API key for authentication to the model endpoint you configure. The current workflow does not use your favorites or private notes as model input.
  • Proxies: If you enable a proxy, the relevant connections pass through it according to your settings. The proxy handles connection information and any proxy authentication details you configure.

These requests originate on your device and do not pass through a ScholarReel developer relay. Providers and websites receive network information needed for the request, such as an IP address, and process received information under their own policies. Their storage, logging and retention practices depend on the service. Consider whether your research descriptions contain private or unpublished material when selecting a model, source or proxy.

Apple purchases

Apple processes the one-time purchase. The app uses StoreKit to verify purchase status, unlock features and restore purchases. It requires no ScholarReel account and does not read your payment card details. Apple handles purchase and payment information under its applicable policies.

Local web and HTTP service

The web service is enabled by default. While the app is in the foreground, the addresses shown in Settings provide access to local papers, briefs, notes, favorites, research settings, non-secret configuration, task history and related management actions.

The current service has no access password and uses unencrypted HTTP. Devices that can reach it can access these materials and functions. Keys and passwords entered through the website also travel over that HTTP connection; storing them in Keychain does not encrypt that transfer. Use a trusted network or disable the service in Settings → Advanced Settings → HTTP & Web Service. The service pauses when the app goes into the background.

Retention, export and deletion

The app keeps local material for later use. You can edit notes, remove favorites, delete research or model configurations, and clear saved keys or passwords in model or proxy settings. Deleting a topic or source does not delete existing papers or briefs.

Trash retains papers and their identifiers to filter repeated recommendations and allows restoration. Moving a paper to Trash is not permanent deletion. JSON exports include the library, reading data, Trash and research configuration. App-managed model API keys and proxy passwords are excluded.

To remove the entire local paper library, use the system’s Delete App action; Offload App retains documents and data. Keychain records may remain after deleting the app, so clear unwanted keys and passwords in the app first. Exported copies, system backups and information retained by third-party services must be managed separately. Deleting local material does not delete those copies.

Support and policy updates

When you contact support, we receive the email address, issue description and attachments you choose to provide and use them to respond and address the issue. Do not send API keys or passwords. For privacy questions or requests concerning information you have sent to support, contact luo980@gmail.com.

If the app’s data handling changes, we will update this policy and its effective date.

This support website

This support website is hosted on GitHub Pages. It contains no added analytics scripts, advertising, or submission forms. GitHub Pages logs and stores visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes; GitHub processes hosting-related access data under its privacy policy. Email links open your chosen mail application.
